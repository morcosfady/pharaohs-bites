Our Kitchen

A family kitchen,
three generations deep

Pharaoh's Bites began with one wood-fired oven, a hand-written book of recipes, and a family that refused to shorten a single step. Half a century later the oven is still lit before dawn, the feteer is still stretched by hand, and the recipes have not changed a word.

We grind our own spices, press our own tahini, and bake bread every twenty minutes from five-thirty in the morning until the last order goes out. Everything that reaches you was made in this building, today, by someone whose name we know.
