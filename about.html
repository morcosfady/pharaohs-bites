Who We Are

Pharaoh's Bites is a family
before it is a business

Pharaoh's Bites is a home kitchen in Dallas, TX, run by an Egyptian family who wanted to share the food they grew up cooking. There's no dining room, no storefront and no walk-in counter — every order is cooked to order and sent straight to you.

Every dish on the menu is 100% plant-based — no meat, chicken or fish — made with the same recipes and the same care as if it were for our own table.

Heritage

Everything here has a source

Nothing at Pharaoh's Bites was invented in a test kitchen. Every dish came from family recipes, made the same way at home for years before we ever sold a single order.

Handcrafted

Paper-thin layers, not one of them optional

Feteer meshaltet is a test. The dough is rested, then stretched by hand — not rolled — until it's paper-thin. It's folded with homemade butter between every layer, and the moment it's rushed you can taste it.

01

Rest

The dough rests before it's touched again. No shortcuts.

02

Stretch

Stretched by hand until it's paper-thin. No rolling pin, ever.

03

Fold

Homemade butter between every layer, folded by hand.

04

Bake

Baked until it's golden and the layers separate cleanly.
